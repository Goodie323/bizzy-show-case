Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Bizzy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our WhatsApp commerce platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us via WhatsApp, including:

  • Phone number and business name
  • Bank account details for settlement
  • Order history and inventory data
  • Chat messages required to facilitate transactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to process orders, manage inventory, generate digital receipts, settle payments via Paystack, and deliver transactional messaging through Meta.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share necessary transaction details only with trusted service partners:

  • Paystack — for secure payment processing and settlement
  • Meta / WhatsApp — for automated message delivery

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain transaction data for up to 6 years to ensure tax and legal compliance. You may request account deletion and removal of associated personal data at any time.

5. Security

We employ industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) for all data in transit and restrict database access exclusively to authenticated microservices.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data removal requests, email us directly at privacy@bizzy.africa.
