Data Deletion

Last updated: September 2026

You reserve full rights to request the removal of your personal data and account records from Bizzy at any time.

Option 1: Request via WhatsApp

Send a WhatsApp message to our official support line with the text "Delete My Data" along with your registered phone number. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm deletion directly on WhatsApp.

Option 2: Request via Email

Email our privacy team at privacy@bizzy.africa detailing your registered phone number and business name.

Note on Regulatory Retention: Historical transaction logs necessary for auditing, legal compliance, and tax purposes may be retained in fully anonymized form for up to 6 years.
