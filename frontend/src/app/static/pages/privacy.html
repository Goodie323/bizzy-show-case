Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Bizzy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our WhatsApp commerce platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us via WhatsApp, including:

  • Phone number and business name
  • Bank account details for settlement
  • Order history and inventory data
  • Chat messages to facilitate transactions

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, manage inventory, generate receipts, settle payments via Paystack, and deliver WhatsApp messages via Meta.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share necessary transaction details with:

  • Paystack — for payment processing and settlement
  • Meta / WhatsApp — for message delivery

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain transaction data for 6 years for tax and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us or using the Data Deletion page linked below.

5. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.3) for data in transit and restrict database access to authenticated services only.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@bizzy.africa.
